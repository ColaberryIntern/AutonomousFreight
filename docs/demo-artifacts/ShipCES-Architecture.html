1. Layered architecture (own the brain, rent the senses)

Four domain layers hold the logic; the Sense Layer adapters are engine-swappable. EDI 910 tender OMS to TMS, EDI 214 milestones inside TMS, EDI 210 invoice BMS to customer.

2. Lifecycle state machine (canonical shipment record, 15 states)

LIFECYCLE_STATES verbatim. OMS owns RECEIVED..TENDERED, TMS owns SOURCING..DELIVERED, BMS owns BILL_READY..INVOICED. Every transition is audited.
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> RECEIVED
  RECEIVED --> PARSED: parse
  PARSED --> PRICED: price
  PRICED --> QUOTE_SENT: send_quote
  QUOTE_SENT --> WON: win
  QUOTE_SENT --> LOST: lose
  WON --> TENDERED: tender
  TENDERED --> SOURCING: accept (EDI 910)
  SOURCING --> CARRIER_ASSIGNED: assign_carrier
  CARRIER_ASSIGNED --> DISPATCHED: 214 X3
  DISPATCHED --> IN_TRANSIT: 214 AF
  IN_TRANSIT --> DELIVERED: 214 D1
  DELIVERED --> BILL_READY: bill_ready
  BILL_READY --> INVOICED: invoice (EDI 210)
  LOST --> [*]
  INVOICED --> [*]
  EXCEPTION --> RECEIVED: reopen
